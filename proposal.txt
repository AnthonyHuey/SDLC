
Project Overview:
Track the populaton of various species overtime and the influence they have on
each other. 

Implementation:
A std::map to store which type of animal, herbivore & carnivore, which is categorized 
into 3 seperate pools of young, adult, or old. each category will conatain 
the species and age of various animals. 
The type of animal will be the map's key.
The array with the categorized animals will be the map's value.

Simulated events:
Simulation will focus on predation, and repoduction and how each type of animals
population influences the other's. As well as how weather or other natural events
influence both. 

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

   1. It needs to be a simulation.
   2. The simulation can be anything in life or nature.
   3. The simulation needs to be a discrete-event simulation (time-based).
   4. The simulation must feature an std::map.
   5. The simulation must retrieve data from an external file.
   6. The value in the map's key-value pair must be a 3-element std::array of std::lists. This makes a nested data structure, with the outer map hosting an inner array of three lists.
   7. There must be at least 100 lines of data processed by the program. LLM's are well-known for helping to generate data like this.
   8. The driver program should neatly display the std::map data.
   9. The driver should show the initial state of the environment, and how it changes after a certain number of time periods. Use at least 25 time periods.
   10 The program must have at least one function.
